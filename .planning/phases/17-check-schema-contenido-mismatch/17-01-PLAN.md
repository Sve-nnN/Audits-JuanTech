<objective>
Implement the SD-06 site-level check that flags pages declaring high-risk JSON-LD types (`FAQPage`, `HowTo`, `Product`+`AggregateRating`, `Review`) without a matching visible-content signal in the raw HTML, always at `warning` severity, and wire the shared `renderVerdictByPageId` contract so a later plan (17-02) can suppress false positives on pages confirmed CSR by the v1.2 render sample.

Purpose: closes SCHEMA-06/SCHEMA-07 — the risk of a Google manual action for "misleading structured data" when JSON-LD promises content (FAQ answers, HowTo steps, ratings, reviews) that isn't actually visible to users.
Output: `schemaContentMismatchCheck` (SiteCheck, checkId `SD-06`) registered in `schemaSiteChecks`, plus the `renderVerdictByPageId` field on `SiteCheckCtx`/`RunAllChecksOptions` that 17-02 will populate from the worker's render pass.

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|------------------|
| T-17-01 | Information Disclosure | `contentMismatch.ts` measuredValue/recommendation strings | accept | Only echoes JSON-LD `@type` labels (public schema.org vocabulary), never raw page content/PII — consistent with existing schema checks (schemaTypes.ts, schemaValidate.ts). |
